# Privacy
Euro-Office Lite works offline. It has no accounts, no telemetry and no analytics, and it does not send your documents anywhere.

The only network request the app makes is when you paste or insert an image that points to a web address: the app downloads that image so it can be placed in your document. Nothing else is sent.

Your documents, recent files list and recovery data stay on your computer. While a document is open, its temporary files are kept in your system's  temporary folder.
